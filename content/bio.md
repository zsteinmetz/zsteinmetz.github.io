### Unraveling pollutant pathways in agroecosystems through robust measurement and reproducible data analysis

I investigate the fate and effects of anthropogenic pollutants across soils, agriculture, and connected land–water systems. My [work](/research) combines environmental chemistry, instrumental analysis, and reproducible data workflows to understand how pollutants move, transform, persist, and affect ecosystems.

This spans analytical method development and quality assurance, environmental monitoring, data interpretation, and transparent scientific communication.
